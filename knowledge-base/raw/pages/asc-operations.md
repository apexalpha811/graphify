# ASC Operations

Ambulatory Surgery Centers (ASCs) are outpatient surgical facilities operating independently from hospitals. They are cost-efficient, physician-friendly, and increasingly where elective and same-day procedures migrate as payer pressures grow.

## Core Operational Pillars

### Revenue Cycle Management
ASC billing is distinct from physician group billing. Key requirements:
- Facility-fee-specific RCM infrastructure
- Implant capture from OR logs
- Case rate contract management
- CMS ASC Payment System compliance (updated annually)
- Payer contracting: know your rates per CPT code, negotiate case rates for high-implant specialties

Top ASC billing companies 2026: Waystar, Greenway Health, AdvancedMD, MediBill MD.

### Scheduling and OR Utilization
- Block scheduling: assign OR blocks to high-volume surgeons, protect and audit utilization
- Minimize turnover time between cases (target under 20 minutes)
- Same-day cancellation tracking: identify root causes (patient no-show, incomplete pre-op, surgeon lateness)
- Case mix: balance high-margin cases (spine, ortho, ophthalmology) with volume drivers

### Compliance and Accreditation
Three accrediting bodies: AAAHC, Joint Commission, AAASF. Each requires:
- Ongoing quality improvement programs
- Infection control protocols
- Credentialing and privileging of all physicians
- Emergency protocols and equipment checks
- Annual policy review

CMS Conditions for Coverage (42 CFR Part 416) is the federal floor. State regs may be stricter.

### Staffing
- Administrator: runs day-to-day ops, owns P&L
- Nurse Manager / DON: clinical oversight, staffing, QAPI
- Circulator RNs + Scrub Techs: core OR staff
- PACU nurses: post-op recovery
- Business Office: billing, scheduling, insurance verification, prior auth

Turnover is the #1 cost driver. Invest in retention: competitive pay, flexible scheduling, tuition reimbursement.

### Technology Stack
Purpose-built ASC platforms (not hospital EMRs):
- **Surgical Information Systems (SIS)**: scheduling, clinical documentation, billing
- **Amkai / Provation**: popular mid-market options
- **Ambula**: newer, cloud-native
- Analytics: track case volume, OR utilization %, payer mix, supply cost per case

## Key Metrics to Track
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| OR utilization | >70% |
| Case cancellation rate | <5% |
| Turnover time | <20 min |
| Days in A/R | <35 |
| Net collection rate | >95% |
| Infection rate | 0% |

## 2026 Trends
- Migration of spine, total joints, and cardiac cath to ASC settings as CMS expands approved procedure list
- AI-assisted scheduling and prior authorization
- Value-based contracts replacing pure fee-for-service
- Private equity consolidation (USPI, SCA Health, AmSurg dominate multi-site ownership)
