# Film Production

Filmmaking workflow from development through delivery. Covers both traditional and AI-assisted pipelines.

## Production Phases

### Pre-Production
Everything before cameras roll (or prompts run).
- **Script development**: story, structure, coverage, rewrites
- **Breakdown**: scene-by-scene, identify locations, cast, props, wardrobe, VFX needs
- **Scheduling**: shooting schedule from breakdown (1-liner, full schedule). Tool: StudioBinder, Celtx, Gorilla
- **Budgeting**: top sheet → detailed budget. Lock before production starts
- **Casting**: sides, auditions, callbacks, offers, deals
- **Locations**: scouts, permits, agreements, tech scouts
- **Storyboards / shot list**: visual plan for every scene. Now AI-assisted (Midjourney, DALL-E for concept boards)
- **Crew**: department heads first (DP, AD, production designer, sound), then crew

### Production
Principal photography.
- **Call sheet**: daily bible. Scenes shooting, locations, call times, crew, cast, equipment
- **1st AD**: runs the set, protects the schedule, calls action/cut
- **DP**: camera and lighting. Works with director on visual language
- **Sound**: production audio is sacred. Bad audio kills a good picture
- **Script supervisor**: continuity, coverage notes, editor's friend
- **Daily workflow**: shoot → download/backup footage → sync audio → dailies review

### Post-Production
- **Editorial**: offline edit (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Avid). Picture lock before anything else moves
- **VFX**: compositing, CGI, cleanup. After picture lock
- **Color**: DI (digital intermediate) — grade for look, platform specs
- **Sound**: dialogue edit, ADR, SFX, Foley, music, mix (5.1, stereo, Atmos)
- **Delivery**: specs vary by platform (Netflix, theatrical, broadcast, web). H.264/H.265 for most; ProRes for broadcast

## 2026 AI-Assisted Workflow
AI has entered every phase. Cost reductions of 60-90% on some workflows.
- **Script to storyboard**: Midjourney/DALL-E 3 for concept art and boards in hours, not days
- **Text-to-video**: Sora, Kling, Runway Gen-3 for b-roll, inserts, and conceptual sequences
- **AI voices**: ElevenLabs for scratch audio, ADR temp tracks, documentary narration
- **Music**: Suno, Udio for temp score or low-budget final
- **Editing assistance**: AI rough cuts (Descript, Adobe Premiere AI), transcription-based editing
- **Color**: AI-assisted matching and looks

## Independent Film Principles
- Lock the script before spending money
- Budget contingency: 10% minimum
- The AD protects the schedule. Respect the AD
- Sound problems are expensive to fix in post. Fix them on set
- Multiple backup drives. Footage loss is unrecoverable
- Relationships are the real currency — crews return for the next project
