# Medical Aesthetics

Medical aesthetics covers minimally invasive and non-invasive cosmetic procedures performed in clinical settings. In 2026 it is one of the fastest-growing healthcare segments, driven by GLP-1 side effects, an aging population, and social media demand.

## Core Treatment Categories

### Injectables
- **Neuromodulators (Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Daxxify)**: wrinkle relaxation, brow lifting, jaw slimming, hyperhidrosis, migraine. Daxxify lasts 6+ months vs Botox's 3-4 months.
- **Dermal Fillers (Juvederm, Restylane, Sculptra, Radiesse)**: volume restoration, lip augmentation, jawline definition, under-eye hollows
- **Bioremodelers (Profhilo, polynucleotides/PN, next-gen HA boosters)**: stimulate fibroblast activity, rebuild collagen from within — not volumizing, skin quality focus
- **Biostimulators (Sculptra, Radiesse)**: collagen induction, longer-lasting than HA fillers

### Energy-Based Devices
- **Radiofrequency (RF)**: Morpheus8, Thermage — skin tightening, collagen remodeling
- **Ultrasound (HIFU)**: Ultherapy — deep tissue lifting
- **Laser**: CO2 resurfacing, IPL for pigment/vascular, Halo hybrid fractional
- **Body contouring**: CoolSculpting (cryolipolysis), Emsculpt (muscle + fat), truSculpt

### Threads
- **PDO threads**: lifting and repositioning, especially mid-face and jowls. High demand for "Ozempic face" correction (rapid weight loss from GLP-1s leaves volume loss and sagging)

### Skincare
- Medical-grade skincare lines: SkinMedica, Obagi, ZO Skin Health, Alastin
- Chemical peels: VI Peel, Jessner, TCA
- Microneedling: standalone or with PRP/exosomes

## 2026 Trends
- **GLP-1/Ozempic face**: rapid weight loss driving demand for mid-face volumization, PDO thread repositioning, and neck/body skin laxity treatments
- **Combination packages**: 40-60% higher per-patient revenue. Micro-dosed Botox + bioremodeler + filler + threads staged over 3-4 visits
- **Recurring revenue models**: memberships, prepaid treatment series, subscription skincare — clients treating aesthetics as ongoing wellness
- **Regenerative aesthetics**: PRP, exosomes, polynucleotides gaining clinical evidence
- **Men's aesthetics**: fastest-growing patient demographic
- **AI-assisted consultations**: 2D/3D imaging integrated with practice management systems

## Practice Management
- **Consultation flow**: photos, skin analysis, goals → treatment plan → package presentation
- **Key software**: Nextech, PatientNow, Aesthetic Record, Symplast
- **Regulatory**: FTC and FDA oversight tightening on device claims and off-label use. Scope-of-practice enforcement varies by state (NP/PA injection authority)
- **Pricing strategy**: rising costs (filler prices up 15-20% since 2023) forcing menu repricing and supply negotiation
